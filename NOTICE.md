## Feedback sounds

The recording start and stop sounds (`marimba_start.wav`, `marimba_stop.wav`,
`pop_start.wav`, `pop_stop.wav`) are our own synthesis. They are generated by a
script rather than recorded or sampled, so they contain no third-party material
